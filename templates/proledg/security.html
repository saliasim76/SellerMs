A single locked engine no module can route around

Every voucher in Proledge — sales, purchases, banking, inventory moves, production, payroll — posts through one locked 20-parameter double-entry engine. No screen and no module gets its own back door, so the books can't drift out of balance.

A universal exclusion rule means cancelled and reversal vouchers drop out of every report, but the audit trail stays intact. The result: your Trial Balance, master balances and every report reconcile to the same number.

{{ icon('lock') }} ACCOUNT & DEVICE SECURITY

Designed around the lost-laptop threat

The realistic risk for a small business isn't a nation-state — it's a laptop left in a taxi with a session still open. Proledge is built for exactly that. Turn on TOTP two-factor auth with any authenticator app, and sessions stay short — about four hours — so an abandoned device doesn't stay logged in for long.

Aggressive no-cache headers mean hitting the browser's Back button after sign-out won't resurrect a financial screen, and logins are session-only. Invoices and documents open inline as view-only PDFs, with downloads locked down under owner control. Enterprise teams can also require single sign-on with Microsoft Entra — so access follows your own identity provider, and a departing employee loses access the moment they're off-boarded, with no separate password to revoke.

{{ icon('cloud') }} INFRASTRUCTURE & DATA

Hosted, backed up and isolated per tenant

Proledge is a cloud-hosted SaaS running on enterprise cloud infrastructure with managed backups, so you're not responsible for patching a server or scheduling your own database dumps. Attachments and source documents are stored with short-lived secure links and long-term retention.

Every company's data is isolated per tenant: each company keeps its own books, currency and chart of accounts, and screens and reports are scoped to the company you're working in.
